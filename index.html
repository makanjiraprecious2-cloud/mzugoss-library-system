Register One Book

Barcode scanner: Connect a USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner, click the barcode field and scan the book. Most scanners act like a keyboard and automatically type the barcode followed by Enter.

If the barcode is already registered in this system, the title, author and subject will be retrieved automatically.
Automatic shelf arrangement: the system places the book in the correct department shelf (Technical, Science, Language or Humanities). Within each department, Form 4 is the top level, followed by Form 3, Form 2 and Form 1 at the bottom. Books at the same level are arranged alphabetically.

Register Students From File

Upload a CSV or TXT file containing the student Name and Form. Student numbers are generated automatically and every imported student automatically receives an account.

Required columns: Name, Form
Example: Name,Form
John Banda,Form 1

Student numbers are created automatically as MZG/0001, MZG/0002, etc. An old Number column may be present, but the system will generate a new number automatically.

Default login: Library Number + default password MZUGOSS123. Students can change their password and profile photo after logging in.

Register Books From File

Upload a CSV or TXT file containing book details. The system automatically creates the catalogue records.

Column order: Full Name of Book, Author, Category, Barcode, Book Number, Class/Form, Shelf Number, Shelf Section, Shelf Level, Shelf Position, Quantity
Required: Full Name of Book, Author, Barcode
Example: Full Name of Book,Author,Category,Barcode,BookNumber,Class,ShelfNumber,ShelfSection,ShelfRow,ShelfPosition,Quantity
Things Fall Apart,Chinua Achebe,Literature,LIT003,LIB-001,Form 1,,,,,5

Duplicate barcodes are skipped so existing catalogue records are not overwritten.
